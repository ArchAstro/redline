Data handling

Review before connecting

Redline handles webpage data only when you use it on a site you enable.

What it handles
Selected text and comments; the page URL, title, and nearby DOM context; and optional screenshots only when you separately enable full visual mode.
Where it stays
Submitted feedback stays in the local Redline sidecar. Redline schedules in-progress Chrome drafts and temporary screenshots for deletion after seven days and removes expired drafts the next time Chrome runs.
Who receives it
No data or telemetry is sent to ArchAstro. When you invoke your coding agent, selected data may be transferred to your configured coding-model provider under that provider's terms.
Your controls
You can delete individual feedback or use Clear all local Redline data. Disconnecting stops this browser without deleting submitted feedback.
