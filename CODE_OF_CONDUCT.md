# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Redline to be safe, respectful, and productive for
everyone. Be considerate, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and
focus disagreement on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to security@archastro.ai. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and may
remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed. Maintainers who are involved in a report will not participate in its
resolution.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, and
other official Redline community spaces.
